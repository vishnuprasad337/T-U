About The T&U

Welcome to T&U

A Hotel With Munnar Around It

T&U was planned as a comfortable base for travellers coming to Munnar to see the hills, spend time with family and take a break from the usual routine.

The property takes cues from Munnar's plantation and colonial history. Wood details, warm interiors and traditional touches give the rooms a connection to the region while keeping the facilities practical for a modern stay.

The location is useful too.

T&U sits on Mattupetty Road, with access towards Mattupetty, Echo Point, Kundala and Top Station. Munnar town is also nearby, so guests can combine sightseeing with time at the hotel instead of spending the whole day on the road.

There are reasons to stay in for an evening as well.

For travellers comparing a luxury hotel in Munnar, these practical details can matter just as much as the setting.
