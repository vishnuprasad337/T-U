About MaxiMunnar T&U Leisure Hotel

A Comfortable Stay in the Hills of Munnar

Welcome to MaxiMunnar T&U Leisure Hotel, a four-star hotel on Mattupetty Road, Munnar. We created the hotel with one simple thought in mind — give guests a comfortable place to stay while they enjoy everything that makes Munnar special.

T&U has 30 rooms and suites, along with an in-house restaurant, recreation spaces, fitness facilities and other everyday comforts. Being on Mattupetty Road also makes it easy to plan visits to places such as Mattupetty, Echo Point, Kundala and Top Station.

There is a quiet charm to the hotel too. The interiors take a few cues from Munnar's colonial and plantation history, while the rooms have the modern comforts you would expect from a good hotel. Step outside and you have tea gardens, green valleys and the cool mountain air that brings many travellers to Munnar in the first place.

MaxiMunnar T&U Leisure Hotel is a four-star hotel on Mattupetty Road in Munnar, Kerala, offering 30 rooms and suites along with dining, recreation, fitness, event and guest-support facilities.

The hotel is situated on Mattupetty Road, Munnar, along one of the routes used to reach attractions such as Mattupetty, Echo Point, Kundala and Top Station. Munnar town is also within easy reach.

T&U combines 30 rooms and suites with an in-house multi-cuisine restaurant, recreation centre, kids' zone, fitness facilities, outdoor lawn, conference spaces and travel assistance. Its Mattupetty Road location is also useful for guests planning sightseeing around eastern Munnar.
