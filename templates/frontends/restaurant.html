Top Station | A Multi-Cuisine Restaurant in Munnar

Top Station is the multi-cuisine restaurant at MaxiMunnar T&U Leisure Hotel on Mattupetty Road, Munnar. With buffet and à la carte dining, the restaurant serves Indian, South Indian, Gujarati, Jain, Arabic, Chinese and Continental food options.

Large glass windows bring the surrounding hills into the dining area, giving guests a pleasant setting for breakfast, lunch or dinner after a day exploring Munnar.

If you are searching for the best restaurant in Munnar, Top Station offers a convenient dining option with a wide choice of cuisines, an accessible location and a comfortable setting inside T&U Leisure Hotel. It is also a practical choice for families and groups with different food preferences.
